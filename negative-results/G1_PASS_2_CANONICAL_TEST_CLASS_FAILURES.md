|---|---|---|---|

## Retained lesson

EMC is auditable on the canonical test class only when the record preserves boundary membership, channel provenance, intervention mappings, maintained variables, viability semantics and relevant event currents. Boundary relativity is not itself a failure; **unrecorded provenance is**.

The remaining G1 blocker is selection of a minimal nonredundant organization-component family beyond maintenance that survives the established counterexample programme.
