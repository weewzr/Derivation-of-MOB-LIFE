# G1 Specialist — Endogenous Maintenance: Informative Failures

These failures constrain any later canonical maintenance definition. They do not show that the candidate quantities are useless.

| ID | Candidate weakened/rejected as sufficient | Counterexample / failure mode | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| NR-G1-EM-01 | viability persistence / return probability alone | equilibrium crystal or frozen structure | persistence can be passive; require nonzero challenge plus active renewal/restoration |
| NR-G1-EM-02 | nonequilibrium current / entropy production alone | convection or externally driven open CRN | driven flux does not identify maintenance |
| NR-G1-EM-03 | state-dependent feedback alone | externally thermostatted or feedback-controlled machine | restorative control can originate outside `mathcal B`; provenance of control channels is required |
| NR-G1-EM-04 | turnover / replacement alone | externally repaired machine | replacement can be imposed through an external repair channel |
| NR-G1-EM-05 | internal restoration current alone (RCC) | internally controlled nonliving machine | RCC can certify endogenous regulation but cannot identify life/organization |
| NR-G1-EM-06 | RAF/autocatalysis alone | simple RAF without maintained compartment | catalytic closure does not entail boundary maintenance |
| NR-G1-EM-07 | dependency-cycle / graph closure alone | coarse-grained or arbitrarily aggregated dependency graph | representation choice can create/destroy apparent closure |
| NR-G1-EM-08 | endogenous-maintenance certificate as life criterion | fully internally self-repairing engineered machine | endogenous maintenance is weaker than life; do not smuggle later organization criteria into G1 maintenance |
| NR-G1-EM-09 | finite-horizon turnover requirement without scale audit | essential component with turnover time much longer than `T` | false negatives arise if observation horizon is not matched to component time scales |
| NR-G1-EM-10 | maintenance verdict assumed invariant under observation map | coarse-graining hides controller/renewal variables | maintenance provenance and dependency closure require explicit observation-map audits |

## Specialist consequence

**[CAND]** A useful G1 maintenance criterion must combine path-level restoration/renewal with explicit boundary/control provenance and dependency structure. Even that conjunction remains boundary-, representation- and time-scale-relative.

**[OPEN]** The surviving obstruction is not whether active renewal can be written mathematically; it can. The obstruction is selecting and preserving, without circularity, the boundary, channel types, maintained variables, dependency granularity and time scales under which an endogenous-maintenance certificate is identifiable.
